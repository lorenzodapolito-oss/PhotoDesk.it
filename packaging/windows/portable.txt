PhotoDesk portable mode

While this file sits next to the program (PhotoDesk.exe), PhotoDesk keeps its settings in the
PhotoDeskData folder beside it and writes nothing to %APPDATA%. Copy the whole folder to move
PhotoDesk with its settings.

Delete this file to use %APPDATA%\PhotoDesk instead.

Put DarkRaw and LightRaw in folders named DarkRaw and LightRaw next to PhotoDesk.exe (or set their
paths in PhotoDesk > Impostazioni) and PhotoDesk finds them on its own.
